Privacy policy

Centilth for iPhone, iPad, Mac and the centilth.com website · Last updated: 22 November 2026

This is a translation for your convenience. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

This policy complies with Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Personal Data Protection (LOPDGDD).

1. In short

  • We don't collect data. Your budgets, transactions and projects are stored on your device and in your iCloud, never on our servers.
  • You don't need an account to use the app.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. We don't sell or share data with anyone.
  • No connection to your bank and no third-party AI. The app never asks for your bank credentials and doesn't send your data to AI services.

2. Data controller

Gregorio Castaño Lizcano, Spanish tax ID 07487035Y. Contact: grcasliz@gmail.com.

3. Where your data is

  • In your iCloud: your budgets are stored in your own iCloud account (iCloud Drive › Centilth › Presupuestos) so you have them on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, and backups in iCloud Drive › Centilth › Copias_de_seguridad. It's your storage, managed by Apple with your Apple ID; we have no access to it.
  • On your device: each device also keeps a copy so it works offline. If iCloud Drive is turned off, data and backups stay only on the device.
  • When you choose to share: if you export a project or an Excel file, it goes wherever you choose in the Share menu (Files, Mail, etc.).

4. Subscription

The Centilth Pro subscription is managed by Apple with your Apple ID. Apple processes the payment; we don't receive your name, email or payment details. The app only asks your device whether the subscription is active. Apple's processing is governed by its privacy policy.

7. Your rights

Because your data is on your device and in your iCloud, you're in control: you can export or delete it at any time. To delete everything, remove the app from your devices and the Centilth folder from iCloud Drive (or in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage). For the data in the table above, you can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to grcasliz@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month at most. You can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with the data protection authority of your country.

8. Security

  • Your data only travels between your devices and your iCloud, encrypted by Apple; it never passes through our servers.
  • On each device, it's protected by the device's encryption and passcode.
  • You can make backups whenever you want from Settings.

9. If Centilth changes owner

If the app passed to another owner, your data would stay on your device and in your iCloud, because it's never in our hands. Only the data in the table above (support emails) would be transferred, if applicable, and we'd let you know beforehand, with the option to ask for it to be deleted.

10. The centilth.com website

The website only presents the app: it has no accounts or forms. When you visit it, the server logs technical data (IP, browser, date and time) to serve it and protect it against abuse, on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR), and deletes it within 30 days at most. Until centilth.com is available, these pages are published on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which may log visitors' IP addresses for security and processes data under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The centilth.com website will be hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany, European Union). Both act as data processors. It doesn't use analytics, ads or third-party cookies; it only remembers your theme and language in your browser (see the cookie policy, in Spanish).

11. Minors and changes

The app is not intended for children under 14, the minimum age to consent to data processing in Spain (Art. 7 LOPDGDD), or the higher age set in your country. If the way we process data changes, we'll update this policy and let you know in the app before applying it.
