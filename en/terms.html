4. Free and Pro plans

  • Free, forever: up to 20 spending categories (income categories and the reconciliation one don't count), 1 project, history for the current month and the previous one, and a weekly backup. No Reconcile, Plan, Stats, Optimise or Excel export.
  • Pro: unlimited categories, Reconcile, Plan, Stats and Optimise with your full history, Excel export, unlimited projects, daily, weekly or monthly backups and the Mac app. €2.99/month or €19.99/year (prices in Spain, VAT included; they may vary by country).
